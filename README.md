# Problem Definition
- Business Context
>	Spar Rwanda is a wholesale distributor serving all provinces of Rwanda, including Kigali, southern, northern, eastern, and western provinces. The company supplies food and beverage products like Inyange dairy products, Bralirwa beverages, Leffe beer, and Doritos snacks to HoReCa clients.
- Data Challenges
>	Sales transactions are generated across multiple regions and involve various products and customers. Without advanced SQL analysis, management struggles to track regional product performance, monitor sales growth over time and identify high value customers for targeted business strategies.
- Expected Outcome
>	Analyze sales data to identify top-selling products by region, evaluate monthly sales trends, measure growth performance and segment customers based on purchasing behavior to support strategic sales and inventory decisions.
